# Astrology Remedies Consultation

When you look at a Vedic birth chart, you are looking at a map of past karma manifesting in the present. While some areas of the chart indicate smooth sailing, others highlight deeply ingrained blockages, delays, and frustrations. The Astrology Remedies Consultation at Shambhavaa is designed specifically to address these challenging areas of your chart through traditional, disciplined, and practical corrective measures.

We believe that astrology should not just diagnose a problem and leave you in fear; it should offer a pathway to navigate it. However, we strictly maintain that remedies are spiritual tools for alignment, not magical shortcuts to bypass your karma.

## Who This Consultation Is For

This consultation is highly recommended if you:
- Are going through a particularly difficult Dasha or transit (such as Sade Sati) and feel overwhelmed.
- Have identified a specific, recurring blockage in your life (e.g., chronic financial instability, recurring relationship failures) and want to know if there is an astrological remedy.
- Are looking to adopt a disciplined spiritual practice (sadhana) aligned with your planetary energies.
- Want to know which deities, mantras, or lifestyle changes best support your chart's weaknesses.

*Please note: We only prescribe remedies after analyzing your entire chart. If you want a full life reading along with remedies, please book a [Vedic kundli reading](/vedic-kundli-reading).*

## The Framework: Remedies Require Full Chart Analysis

A common mistake in modern astrology is prescribing remedies based on a single planetary placement. For instance, wearing a diamond because Venus is your Sun sign ruler is a flawed approach in Jyotish.

At Shambhavaa, we never prescribe a remedy without checking:
- **The D1 Chart:** To see the functional benefics and malefics for your specific Lagna.
- **Divisional Charts:** To confirm the actual strength and dignity of the planet in question.
- **Dasha and Transit:** To ensure we are not aggravating a planet that is currently acting as a severe malefic in your timeline.

*Learn more about [why astrology remedies need full chart analysis](/resources/why-astrology-remedies-need-full-chart-analysis).*

## Types of Remedies We Suggest

Depending on what your chart requires, we may suggest a combination of the following:

1. **Mantra Therapy:** Sound carries frequency. Chanting specific seed (bija) mantras or Vedic hymns can help harmonize the mind with a difficult planetary energy.
2. **Donation (Daan):** A powerful way to appease malefic planets by willingly giving up resources associated with that planet (e.g., donating food for the Moon, or labor/time for Saturn).
3. **Discipline and Fasting:** Forcing the body and mind into a disciplined state on specific days aligns with the energy of planets like Saturn and the Sun.
4. **Gemstones and Rudraksha:** We are highly conservative with gemstone recommendations. A gemstone amplifies a planet's energy; we only recommend them for functional benefics that are weak but well-placed.

## What Is Not Promised

To protect you from exploitative practices, you must understand our ethical boundaries regarding remedies:
- **No Guaranteed Outcomes:** We do not guarantee that chanting a mantra will get your ex back, instantly secure a job, or cure a disease.
- **No Fear-Based Rituals:** We do not prescribe highly expensive, fear-based poojas. True remedies require *your* discipline and effort, not just your money.
- **Not a Substitute for Action:** An astrological remedy supports your practical efforts; it does not replace the need to study for an exam, apply for a job, or seek medical treatment.
