# Vastu Shastra Consultation

Just as your birth chart reflects your internal karma and timeline, the space you live and work in reflects your external reality. **Vastu Shastra** is the ancient Indian science of architecture and spatial geometry. It teaches us how to align our physical environment with the natural forces of the universe—the five elements (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Space) and the directional energies.

When your environment is out of alignment, it can create invisible friction in your life, leading to chronic health issues, financial drain, or relationship conflicts. A Vastu Consultation at Shambhavaa is designed to identify these structural imbalances and provide practical, non-destructive remedies.

## The Connection Between Vastu and Astrology

Your home is a physical manifestation of your Kundli. For example, if you are experiencing severe financial blockages due to an afflicted 2nd or 11th house in your birth chart, you will almost certainly find a corresponding Vastu defect in the North or Southeast direction of your home. 

By correcting the Vastu defect, you are actively performing a physical remedy for your astrological chart, easing the blockage and allowing the energy to flow.

## What We Analyze

During a comprehensive Vastu consultation, we analyze your floor plan (whether for a residential home, an apartment, or a commercial office) with a focus on:

1. **The Entrance (Main Door):** The most critical element of Vastu, determining what kind of energy (prosperity or struggle) enters the space.
2. **The 16 Vastu Zones:** We map your floor plan into 16 specific zones, identifying where vital activities (sleeping, cooking, working) are taking place.
3. **The Five Elements (Panchamahabhuta):** We check for elemental imbalances. For instance, having a water element (like a sink or blue paint) in the Fire zone (Southeast) can literally "extinguish" your financial flow and cause digestive health issues.
4. **Placement of Objects:** The location of mirrors, heavy furniture, toilets, and altars.
