# Dasha and Transit Reading

A birth chart is a static map of your karma, showing the promises and potentials of your life. However, this map is not activated all at once. The unfolding of your life—the *when* of your karma—is governed by the dynamic systems of Dasha (planetary periods) and Gochar (planetary transits). 

The Dasha and Transit Reading at Shambhavaa is a highly focused consultation dedicated purely to the element of timing. If you already know your chart's general strengths but want to understand exactly what the next 12 to 24 months hold, this is the reading for you.

## Who This Consultation Is For

This specialized timing session is ideal for individuals who:
- Are approaching a major life crossroad and need to know the astrological weather for the coming year.
- Have recently started a new Mahadasha or Antardasha and feel a sudden, confusing shift in their life's focus.
- Are experiencing a major transit, such as Sade Sati (Saturn's transit over the Moon) or a nodal return (Rahu-Ketu), and need guidance.
- Are waiting for a specific event to happen (like a job change or moving abroad) and want to know when the window of opportunity opens.

*If you have never had your chart read before, we highly recommend starting with a [full birth chart reading](/vedic-kundli-reading) before diving purely into timing.*

## What We Check: Dasha (The Operating System)

Vimshottari Dasha is a 120-year cycle divided among the nine planets. Depending on when you were born, you are currently running a specific Mahadasha (major period) and Antardasha (sub-period).

During this reading, we analyze:
- **The Mahadasha Lord:** This planet dictates the overarching theme, mindset, and environment of a multi-year chapter in your life. We check its dignity, house placement, and rulership to understand what it wants to deliver.
- **The Antardasha Lord:** This planet acts as the immediate trigger within the broader Mahadasha, changing the flavor of the current months or years.
- **Pratyantardasha (Sub-sub-period):** We occasionally drill down to this level to pinpoint tighter windows for specific events.

## What We Check: Transit (The Delivery Mechanism)

While Dasha sets the agenda, transits (where the planets are in the sky right now) execute the events. A promise made by the Dasha must be validated by the transiting planets.

We heavily focus on the heavyweights of the zodiac:
- **Saturn (Shani):** Saturn's transit brings reality checks, structure, delay, and hard work to the house it occupies or aspects. We assess its movement to see where you must put in effort.
- **Jupiter (Guru):** Jupiter’s transit brings expansion, grace, opportunity, and protection. We look at where Jupiter is blessing your chart.
- **Rahu and Ketu:** The nodes bring sudden obsession, expansion, or sudden detachment and letting go. We track their 18-month transits to understand where karmic disruption is occurring.

*To understand why these two systems must be synthesized, read [why dasha and transit must be read together](/resources/why-dasha-and-transit-must-be-read-together).*

## The Synthesis

The true power of this reading lies in combining the two. For example, if you are hoping for a career change, we look to see if you are running the Dasha of the 10th lord, and then we check if transiting Saturn or Jupiter is currently activating your 10th house. When both systems say "yes," the event occurs.

## What Is Not Promised

- We do not give exact day-by-day predictions. Astrology highlights windows of time (usually spanning weeks or months).
- We do not use transits to instill fear. A difficult Saturn transit is a period of structuring, not a curse.
- We do not override your free will. A good dasha still requires you to take action.
