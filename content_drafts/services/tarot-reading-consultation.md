# Tarot Reading Consultation

While Vedic astrology (Jyotish) is unparalleled for mapping out your entire life's timeline, karmic promises, and long-term trajectory, sometimes you need immediate clarity on a specific, pressing situation. This is where the **Tarot Reading Consultation** comes in.

Tarot is an intuitive, synchronous tool that acts as a mirror to your current subconscious state and the immediate energetic forces surrounding a specific decision. 

## When to Choose Tarot Over Astrology?

You should book a Tarot reading if:
- You have a highly specific, immediate question (e.g., "What is the hidden dynamic in my current relationship conflict?").
- You are facing a crossroads and need to see the probable outcomes of Choice A vs. Choice B.
- You do not have your exact birth time (which is strictly required for an accurate Vedic astrology reading).
- You want to understand the current energetic blockages stopping you from moving forward.

## Our Approach to Tarot

At Shambhavaa, we do not use Tarot for fortune-telling or deterministic predictions (e.g., "You will win the lottery tomorrow"). We use the cards as a psychological and spiritual diagnostic tool. 

The imagery and archetypes of the Tarot help bypass the logical mind and reveal the hidden fears, desires, and energetic currents that are actually driving your situation. 

During the session, we will:
1. **Clarify the Question:** We will help you frame your question to get the most empowering and actionable answer.
2. **Pull the Spread:** We will draw the cards and explain the narrative they are presenting.
3. **Actionable Guidance:** We will translate the spiritual message into practical steps you can take today to shift the energy in your favor.

## Common Areas Explored

- **Love & Relationships:** Understanding a partner's perspective, healing from a breakup, or identifying what is blocking you from finding love.
- **Career & Business:** Navigating office politics, deciding whether to leave a job, or understanding the energy surrounding a new business venture.
- **Spiritual Growth:** Identifying what shadow work needs to be done or what karmic lesson you are currently moving through.

## What Is Not Promised

- We do not answer questions regarding medical diagnoses, legal outcomes, or financial investments.
- We do not read on third parties without their consent (e.g., "What is my ex doing right now?"). The reading is about *you* and your energetic sphere.
