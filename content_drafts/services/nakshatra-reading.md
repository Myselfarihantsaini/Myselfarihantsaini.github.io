# Nakshatra Reading Consultation

While the 12 Zodiac signs (Rashis) provide the broad environment of your life, the 27 Nakshatras (Lunar Mansions) reveal the micro-details. The Nakshatra Reading Consultation at Shambhavaa is a deep psychological and spiritual dive into the exact cosmic frequencies operating behind the planets in your birth chart.

Vedic astrology is fundamentally a lunar-based system, and the Nakshatras represent the mind, the subconscious patterns, and the deeper karmic motivations driving your actions. If you want to move beyond generic astrology and understand the profound "why" behind your behavior and life events, this reading is for you.

## Who This Consultation Is For

This specialized reading is perfect for individuals who:
- Want a deep psychological analysis of their personality, fears, and subconscious drives.
- Feel that general zodiac sign descriptions do not accurately capture their true nature.
- Want to understand their unique life purpose and the specific themes that will dominate their path.
- Are interested in the mythological and symbolic stories that are playing out in their real life.

## What We Check: The Nakshatra Framework

During this consultation, we do not look at planets merely by the sign they occupy; we zoom in to the exact Nakshatra and Pada (quarter) they sit in. 

- **The Janma Nakshatra (Moon Nakshatra):** This is the most important Nakshatra in your chart. It defines your mental framework, how you process emotions, and your fundamental temperament. It also determines the starting point of your Dasha timeline.
- **The Lagna Nakshatra:** This reveals your physical approach to the world, your outward personality, and how you naturally assert yourself in your environment.
- **Key Planetary Nakshatras:** We analyze the Nakshatras of your Sun, Lagna Lord, and any cluster of planets. If multiple planets sit in a specific Nakshatra (like Ashwini or Revati), the mythology, deity, and symbol of that Nakshatra will heavily dictate your life experiences.

## The Deity, Symbol, and Dasha Connection

Every Nakshatra is ruled by a specific planetary lord and a specific presiding deity, and is represented by a unique symbol. 

For example, a person with the Moon in Anuradha Nakshatra (ruled by Saturn, deity Mitra) will have a life heavily themed around friendship, networking, and overcoming strict boundaries, often achieving success later in life due to Saturn's rulership. 

Furthermore, the Nakshatra lord dictates how a planet behaves. If your Venus is in a Nakshatra ruled by Rahu, your approach to love and relationships will take on Rahu’s obsessive, unconventional qualities. 

## What You Need to Provide

Because Nakshatras span only 13 degrees and 20 minutes, and their Padas span just 3 degrees and 20 minutes, extreme precision is required:
1. Date of Birth
2. Exact Time of Birth (A difference of minutes can change the Lagna Nakshatra Pada)
3. Place of Birth
