# Kundli Matching Consultation

In Vedic culture, marriage is not just the union of two individuals; it is the merging of two karmic timelines. The Kundli Matching Consultation at Shambhavaa goes far beyond the basic computerized point system (Ashtakoot Guna Milan) to provide a deep, realistic, and structural analysis of compatibility between two partners.

A high score out of 36 points does not guarantee a happy marriage, and a low score does not always doom one. True astrological compatibility requires a manual, detailed synthesis of both partners' D1 charts, Navamsa charts, and upcoming Dasha timelines.

## Who This Consultation Is For

This reading is ideal for couples who:
- Are considering marriage (arranged or love) and want to understand their astrological compatibility.
- Want to know if their individual charts support long-term harmony or if there are inherent friction points.
- Have a "Manglik Dosh" or "Rahu-Ketu" mismatch and want an honest, non-fear-based assessment of its actual impact.
- Are already married but facing friction, and want to understand the astrological root of their conflict to navigate it better.

*If you are single and want to know when you will meet a partner, please book a [marriage astrology consultation](/marriage-astrology-consultation).*

## What We Check: Beyond the 36 Points

While we do look at the traditional Guna Milan (points matching) to gauge basic temperamental alignment, our manual analysis focuses heavily on:

- **The Promise of Marriage in Both Charts:** Before matching two charts, we must evaluate the individual promise. If one chart shows severe marital turbulence and the other shows peace, the combination must be handled carefully. We assess the 7th house, 7th lord, and Venus/Jupiter in both D1 charts.
- **Navamsa (D9) Compatibility:** We overlay the Navamsa charts to see if the internal, soul-level realities of both partners align. A strong D1 match with a hostile D9 match often leads to a marriage that looks good to society but is cold behind closed doors.
- **Mental and Emotional Harmony:** We deeply analyze the Moon in both charts. If the Moons are incompatible, the partners will process stress and emotions in entirely different, often clashing, ways.
- **Mars (Manglik) and Nodes:** We look at the placement of Mars to assess the Manglik dosha. We do not use this to create fear; rather, we check if the fiery, assertive energy of one partner is balanced by the other.

*Learn more about the importance of the D9 chart in our resource: [Why Navamsa is important for marriage prediction](/resources/why-navamsa-is-important-for-marriage).*

## Dasha Timelines

Compatibility is also a matter of timing. Two perfectly compatible people can struggle if one enters a deeply isolating Saturn Dasha while the other is in a highly social Venus Dasha. We cross-reference the upcoming Mahadashas for both partners to ensure their life trajectories are moving in complementary directions.

## What Is Not Promised

- **We do not make the final decision for you.** We highlight the strengths and the friction points; the decision to marry is yours.
- We do not guarantee a divorce-free life. Astrology shows the weather, but how you sail the ship together is up to your mutual effort and free will.
- We do not use combinations like Manglik Dosh to scare clients into buying expensive remedies. We offer practical, psychological, and traditional advice on how to handle the energy.
