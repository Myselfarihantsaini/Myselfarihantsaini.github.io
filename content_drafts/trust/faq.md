# Frequently Asked Questions

Before booking a consultation at Shambhavaa, clients often have questions about how Vedic astrology works and what they can expect from a reading. We have compiled detailed answers to our most common inquiries below.

### 1. What details are needed for a kundli reading?
To generate an accurate astrological chart, we require your Date of Birth, exact Time of Birth, and Place of Birth (City, State, and Country). 

### 2. Is exact birth time necessary?
Yes, it is extremely important. While the main D1 chart (Lagna) changes roughly every two hours, the vital divisional charts (like Navamsa for marriage or Dasamsa for career) change every few minutes. An inaccurate birth time can completely alter the timing and accuracy of the predictions.

### 3. Can astrology predict marriage timing?
Astrology can highlight the most probable windows of time when marriage is highly supported by your planetary periods (Dasha) and transits (like Jupiter and Saturn). However, the final outcome always involves your free will and practical effort.

### 4. Can astrology predict career growth?
Yes. By analyzing the 10th house, its lord, the D10 divisional chart, and active dashas, we can identify periods favorable for promotions, job changes, or starting a business. It provides a strategic timeline for your career efforts.

### 5. What is dasha analysis?
Dasha refers to the planetary periods you run throughout your life. Even if a chart promises great wealth or marriage, those events will only manifest when the relevant Dasha (Mahadasha, Antardasha) activates those specific houses in your chart.

### 6. What is transit analysis?
Transit (Gochar) refers to where the planets are physically moving in the sky right now. While Dasha shows the broader period of your life, transits (especially of slow-moving planets like Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu, and Ketu) act as triggers that deliver the results of the Dasha.

### 7. What is Navamsa?
The Navamsa (D9 chart) is a critical divisional chart in Vedic astrology. It is primarily used to assess the strength of the planets, the deeper reality of your marital life, relationships, and the second half of your life. A D1 chart is never read in isolation without the Navamsa.

### 8. What is D10?
The Dasamsa (D10 chart) is the divisional chart specifically used to analyze career, professional status, reputation, and major achievements. It refines the career promises seen in the D1 chart.

### 9. Do remedies work?
Remedies (such as mantras, fasting, and disciplined action) are traditional spiritual tools designed to calm the mind, build focus, and align your energy with planetary frequencies. They work as supportive measures, but they are not magical fixes and do not override the need for practical effort.

### 10. Are predictions guaranteed?
No. There are no absolute guarantees in astrology. The chart shows the karmic map, the probabilities, and the timeline, but human free will, environment, and personal choices play a massive role in the final outcome. 

### 11. Can I ask multiple questions in one consultation?
This depends on the consultation type you book. Specialized sessions (like Career or Marriage) are focused heavily on those specific topics. A Full Birth Chart Reading allows for a broader overview. Please respect the time constraints of your booked session.

### 12. How should I prepare for a consultation?
Ensure you have your accurate birth details ready. Write down your top 2-3 most pressing questions so the astrologer can focus the reading efficiently. Come with an open mind and be prepared to take notes.

### 13. Do you check Rahu-Ketu and Kaal Sarpa?
Yes, we thoroughly analyze the Rahu-Ketu axis, as it dictates karmic desire and detachment. If a Kaal Sarpa formation exists, we assess its actual impact rather than relying on fear-based interpretations, focusing instead on how to navigate its energy constructively.

### 14. Do you provide gemstone or Rudraksha guidance?
Yes, if a chart explicitly requires elemental balancing, we may suggest specific gemstones or Rudraksha. However, this is only done after a strict assessment of the D1 chart and active dashas, as wearing the wrong gemstone can be counterproductive.

### 15. Is astrology a replacement for medical/legal/financial advice?
Absolutely not. Astrology is a tool for self-reflection and spiritual guidance. It should never be used as a substitute for professional medical treatment, legal counsel, or certified financial planning. Please consult respective professionals for serious life matters.
