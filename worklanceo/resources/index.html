Frequently asked questions

Can I hire for multiple locations or in bulk?

Yes, the service model supports multi-location briefs. Specify headcount, shifts, pay and timelines for each location. Actual delivery coverage must be confirmed before engagement.

How do success-based and fixed-fee hiring differ?

Success-based recruitment uses an agreed percentage of annual compensation. A fixed-fee model sets a per-hire amount for defined requirements. Fee triggers, taxes, replacement terms and exclusions should be agreed in writing.

What happens after I submit a hiring requirement?

New hiring requirements are sent to our recruitment team and appear in your workspace. You can see enquiry status updates there. Email and SMS notifications are not yet connected.

Are staffing, payroll and compliance services available now?

They are included as expansion service models. Payroll disbursement, statutory filing, background checks, supplier management and task payouts are not connected to operational providers in this version.
