CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

How to use this platform.

This website is a launch-stage recruitment platform under the name WorkLanceo. The public website accepts enquiries; the administrator dashboard is restricted.

Jobs and hiring

Submitting a profile or hiring requirement shares it with the site’s recruitment administrator for review and follow-up. It does not submit an application to an external employer or create an agency engagement. No candidate is charged through this website.

Commercial terms

Fees, replacement commitments, payment schedules, employment responsibilities, checks and service levels require a separate written agreement. No specific hiring timeline or placement result is guaranteed.

Tools and resources

Calculators use your inputs for estimates. They do not determine statutory liability, process salaries or replace a payroll or compliance specialist. Reference companies have no implied affiliation with this site.

Fair use

Provide accurate information and upload only information you are authorised to share. Use role-related criteria and respectful, non-discriminatory hiring practices.
