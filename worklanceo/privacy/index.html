CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

Your information, handled with care.

This launch-stage notice describes the current website. It must be reviewed and completed with the business’s legal identity, privacy contact, retention policy and grievance process before public launch.

What you choose to save

Sign-in provides an account identifier and display details. Hiring briefs may contain contact details, role requirements and salary budgets. Candidate profiles may contain experience, preferences, contact details and an optional PDF résumé. Checklists store your selected steps.

How your details are used

Your records are stored on the hosted platform and linked to your account so you can view, export or remove them. Submitted requirements and candidate profiles, including contact information and attached résumés, are accessible to the site owner through a restricted recruitment dashboard for review and follow-up. Older private drafts stay private unless you explicitly submit them. Personal checklists remain private. The site does not forward profiles to external employers, send automated marketing messages or process payroll. Do not upload identity documents, bank details or confidential third-party information.

Your controls

Open My workspace to export or remove saved records. Uploaded résumés are accessible to you and, when submitted with your profile, the authorised site administrator. Removing a saved candidate record also deletes its attached résumé. A formal business retention schedule and privacy contact must be added before public launch.

External links and essential access

Reference websites apply their own policies. The sign-in service uses access cookies. No advertising analytics or marketing cookies are added by this site.
