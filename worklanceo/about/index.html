WORK CONNECTS US

A bridge between people and possibility.

WorkLanceo is a recruitment and workforce platform focused on India’s frontline economy. The initial service model centres on permanent recruitment and bulk hiring, with a path toward staffing, payroll coordination and managed workforce services.

Built around clarity

Candidates need honest role details, clear pay and a simple way to express interest. Employers need specific briefs, visible costs and a repeatable hiring process. This site brings those needs into one place.

Current operating status

This is a launch-stage platform. Service descriptions are a proposed operating model, and no placement results or client relationships are claimed. Company registration details, contact channels, service availability and commercial terms must be added by the business before public launch.
