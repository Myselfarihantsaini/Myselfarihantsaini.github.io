Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

At Shambhavaa ("we," "us," or "our"), your privacy is of the utmost importance. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (shambhavaa.com) and when you book an astrology consultation with us.

By accessing the site and using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide our astrology consultation services and improve user experience on our website. This includes:

  • Personal Identification Data: Your name, email address, and phone number (primarily via WhatsApp) when you contact us to book a reading.
  • Astrological Data: Your date of birth, exact time of birth, and place of birth. This data is strictly used to generate your astrological charts (Kundli) for the consultation.
  • Payment Information: When you book a service, payment details may be processed through secure third-party payment gateways. We do not store your credit card or bank account details directly on our servers.
  • Usage Data & Analytics: Information about how you navigate and use our website, including IP addresses, browser types, device information, and pages visited. This helps us optimize our website content.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To generate your Vedic birth charts and provide accurate astrological consultations.
  • To communicate with you regarding your booking, schedule adjustments, and follow-ups.
  • To process payments for our services securely.
  • To analyze website traffic and improve our educational content and user interface.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Protection and Confidentiality

Your birth details and the contents of your astrology consultation are strictly confidential.

  • We do not share, sell, or rent your personal data, birth details, or reading outcomes to third-party marketing agencies.
  • Astrological charts generated for your consultation are used solely for your reading.
  • We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Shambhavaa uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small data files placed on your device.

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function properly.
  • Analytics Cookies: We use tools like Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our site. This helps us identify technical issues and improve content delivery.
  • You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, some parts of our website may not function optimally.

5. Third-Party Tools and Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or utilize third-party tools (such as WhatsApp for communication and third-party gateways for payment). These third parties have their own independent privacy policies. We hold no responsibility or liability for the content and activities of these linked sites or services.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for individuals who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from anyone under the age of 18 without parental consent.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or change our Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us via our Contact Page.
