Marriage Astrology Consultation

Relationships are often the most complex and deeply karmic areas of our lives. The Marriage Astrology Consultation at Shambhavaa is a specialized reading designed to bring clarity to your marital prospects, relationship patterns, and timing.

Whether you are experiencing delays in getting married, facing recurring obstacles in finding the right partner, or simply want to know what your chart promises regarding long-term commitment, this reading provides structured, grounded answers based on traditional Vedic astrology.

Who This Consultation Is For

This reading is highly recommended if you are:

  • Experiencing unexplained delays or obstacles in getting married.
  • Confused about when you are likely to meet a partner or finalize a marriage.
  • Noticing painful, repetitive patterns in your romantic relationships and wanting to understand the karmic root.
  • Wanting to understand the traits and nature of your future spouse.
  • Seeking clarity on whether your chart supports an arranged marriage or a love marriage.

If you already have a partner and want to check compatibility, please see our kundli matching consultation.

What We Check: The D1 Chart Analysis

Marriage is not judged by looking at Venus alone. A comprehensive assessment requires analyzing multiple factors in the root D1 (Rasi) chart:

  • The 7th House and 7th Lord: The 7th house is the primary house of marriage, partnership, and the spouse. We assess its strength, the planets sitting in it, and the condition of the 7th lord to understand your marital promise.
  • The Karakas (Significators): We analyze Venus (the universal significator of romance and relationships) and Jupiter (the significator of the husband in a female's chart). We also look at the Darakaraka (the planet with the lowest degree), which represents the spouse in Jaimini astrology.
  • Relationship Patterns: We look at the 5th house (romance), the 8th house (intimacy and sudden changes), and the 12th house (bed comforts and losses) to understand the psychological dynamics you bring into relationships.

To understand the mechanics behind this, read how marriage timing is checked in Vedic astrology.

The Critical Role of the Navamsa (D9) Chart

The D1 chart shows the physical reality and initial promise, but the Navamsa (D9 chart) is the ultimate reality of marriage.

In this consultation, we meticulously analyze your Navamsa to confirm the strength of the 7th lord and Venus. A strong D1 chart with a severely afflicted Navamsa indicates that a marriage may happen easily but face massive struggles later. Conversely, a delayed marriage in D1 can turn out beautifully if the Navamsa is strong.

Learn more about Navamsa in marriage prediction.

Dasha and Transit Timing

Knowing if you will get married is only half the question; knowing when is the other half.

Marriage does not happen randomly. It occurs when a supportive planetary period (Mahadasha or Antardasha) activates the 7th house, the 7th lord, or Venus/Jupiter. Furthermore, major transits of Jupiter and Saturn must bless the 7th house or Lagna to trigger the event. We will map out your current and upcoming timeline to highlight the most probable windows for marriage.

What You Need to Provide

To ensure accurate analysis—especially of the highly time-sensitive Navamsa chart—we require:

  1. Date of Birth
  2. Exact Time of Birth (Crucial for D9 accuracy)
  3. Place of Birth

What Is Not Promised

Our approach to Jyotish is realistic and ethical. Therefore:

  • We do not guarantee a specific date or name of a spouse.
  • We do not offer magical remedies that will force someone to marry you.
  • We do not claim that an astrological consultation can "fix" a toxic relationship without practical effort and communication on your part.
