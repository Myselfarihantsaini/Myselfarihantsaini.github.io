Career Astrology Consultation

Your professional life consumes a vast amount of your time, energy, and mental focus. When career growth stalls, or when you face toxic work environments and financial instability, it can impact every other area of your life. The Career Astrology Consultation at Shambhavaa is designed to decode your professional karma, helping you align your efforts with your astrological timeline.

Whether you are deciding between a job and a business, aiming for a promotion, or feeling completely lost regarding your life's work, this specialized reading provides structured guidance based on the D1 chart, the D10 Dasamsa, and planetary timing.

Who This Consultation Is For

This reading is tailored for individuals who:

  • Are feeling stagnant in their current job and want to know when a change or promotion is likely.
  • Are confused about which career path, industry, or role suits their natural karmic inclinations.
  • Are debating whether to stay in employment or start their own business.
  • Are facing workplace politics, sudden job losses, or conflicts with authority figures.
  • Are preparing for government exams or competitive roles and want to understand their chances based on their dasha.

What We Check: The D1 Chart Analysis

Career astrology requires a synthesized look at multiple houses in your D1 (Rasi) chart to understand how you work, how you earn, and how you are perceived by the world:

  • The 10th House and 10th Lord: The pinnacle of your chart, representing your career, public reputation, authority, and ultimate achievements.
  • The 6th House: The house of daily routines, employment, service, and overcoming competitors (crucial for jobs and exams).
  • The 2nd and 11th Houses: The 2nd house shows accumulated wealth and resources, while the 11th house shows incoming gains, networks, and the fulfillment of desires.
  • The Amatyakaraka: In Jaimini astrology, this is the planet that acts as your primary "minister," guiding your professional direction and status.

To understand the framework we use, read how career is judged from kundli.

The Crucial D10 (Dasamsa) Chart

You cannot accurately predict professional success without the Dasamsa (D10 chart).

While the D1 chart shows the general promise of wealth and status, the D10 chart zooms in specifically on your professional environment, your actions in society, and the heights you can reach. We analyze the D10 Lagna, the 10th lord of the D10, and planetary dignities within this divisional chart to confirm your career trajectory.

Learn more about how D10 chart helps career prediction.

Dasha and Transit Timing

A career breakthrough happens when the timing is right.

During the consultation, we analyze your current Mahadasha and Antardasha. If you are running the dasha of a well-placed 10th lord, career growth is imminent. If you are running the dasha of the 8th or 12th lord, it may be a period of instability, research, or working behind the scenes. We also overlay current transits—especially Saturn, the karaka of karma and hard work—to identify specific windows for job changes, promotions, or business launches.

(If you only need a quick update on your timeline without a full deep dive, consider a dasha and transit timing session).

What You Need to Provide

Because the D10 chart changes rapidly, accurate birth details are non-negotiable for this reading:

  1. Date of Birth
  2. Exact Time of Birth
  3. Place of Birth

What Is Not Promised

To maintain the integrity of Jyotish, we strictly adhere to the following limitations:

  • We do not guarantee job placements, specific salaries, or exact dates of hire.
  • We do not give specific investment or stock market advice.
  • We do not offer magical remedies to instantly clear government exams—your hard work is the primary requirement.
