Career Astrology Consultation

If you are weighing a job change, another exam attempt, or a move into business, bring that decision to the conversation. Arihant's career consultation explores it through a traditional Jyotish reading alongside the circumstances you describe.

Choose a question to focus on

You might want to discuss your direction, a difficult period at work, or how an earlier reading fits your current situation. It helps to bring the options you actually have, any deadline, and the part you feel unsure about.

What the reading considers

The discussion may include the main birth chart, the D10 divisional chart, and dasha and transit timing. These are interpretive methods within Vedic astrology. Ask how the interpretation follows from the chart and which parts depend on an exact birth time.

For background, read how career is discussed in a kundli reading and the limits of a D10 reading.

What to bring

  • Your birth date, local birth time, and birthplace, including the source of the time.
  • A short outline of your current work or studies.
  • The decision or question you want to discuss.
  • Any uncertain details, rather than a guessed exact time.

For an exam or a job offer

A reading cannot guarantee selection, a salary, or a promotion. Keep eligibility, preparation, contract terms, and employer feedback central to the decision. You should not resign, borrow money, or abandon an application simply because of an astrological prediction.

Before you book

Ask about the fee, session length, format, available times, and whether follow-up questions are included. Confirm any recording arrangements before the conversation. See the consultation process and cancellation terms.
