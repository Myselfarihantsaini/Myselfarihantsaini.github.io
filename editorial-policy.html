Editorial Standards

Editorial Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Shambhavaa aims to publish astrology content that is educational, practical, emotionally grounded, and clear about its limits. We avoid fear-based language, miracle claims, and pressure tactics.

Content Principles

Articles and service descriptions should explain the Jyotish logic behind an interpretation: chart promise, dasha timing, transit influence, house/sign context, and practical reflection. Content should be useful even for readers who do not book a consultation.

Who publishes these pages

Shambhavaa is run by Arihant Saini. About Shambhavaa introduces the consultation practice. The Kundli Mastery Course completion certificate is available in the homepage credentials section; it is a course credential, not evidence that a prediction is certain.

How the writing is prepared

AI tools are used to help draft and edit some website copy, including the September 2026 guide revisions. AI assistance does not establish subject expertise or independent verification. Traditional interpretations, calculation assumptions, and practical examples should be distinguished from demonstrated facts. Examples labelled illustrative are invented to explain a question; they are not client cases.

Sources and corrections

A title in a reading list is not a citation for a claim. Specific quotations should identify the source and edition where available. To report an error, email shambhavaa.reviews@gmail.com with the page URL, sentence, and correction you suggest. Update dates indicate substantive changes to a page, not a new date added to make old writing appear recent.

Client feedback

A testimonial should be supported by the original client message, permission to publish, and a record of the consultation. Sample or AI-generated reviews must not be presented as client experiences. Ratings and client totals should appear only when supporting records are available. We welcome critical as well as positive feedback. The review form prepares an email or WhatsApp message; the visitor must send it to complete submission.

Advertising

Advertising and consultation booking are separate from educational explanations. An advertisement is not an endorsement of its claims. A spiritual interpretation should never be presented as a guarantee of marriage, wealth, healing, or another personal outcome.
