Editorial Standards

Editorial Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

Shambhavaa aims to publish astrology content that is educational, practical, emotionally grounded, and clear about its limits. We avoid fear-based language, miracle claims, and pressure tactics.

Content Principles

Articles and service descriptions should explain the Jyotish logic behind an interpretation: chart promise, dasha timing, transit influence, house/sign context, and practical reflection. Content should be useful even for readers who do not book a consultation.

Author Responsibility

Arihant Saini is responsible for Vedic astrology consultation content on Shambhavaa. Where content is educational, it is written to clarify traditional concepts rather than claim certainty over personal outcomes.

Corrections

If a reader notices an error, unclear claim, outdated detail, or broken link, they can email shambhavaa.reviews@gmail.com. We review correction requests and update pages when appropriate.

Reviews And Testimonials

Testimonials are reviewed before publication. We do not intentionally publish claims that imply guaranteed marriage, wealth, healing, legal victory, or similar outcomes.

Advertising Independence

Ads, affiliate links, or third-party recommendations do not decide the educational stance of Shambhavaa content. Sponsored or external offers should not be treated as professional advice.
