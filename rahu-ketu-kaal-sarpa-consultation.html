Rahu Ketu and Kaal Sarpa Consultation

Of all the celestial entities in Vedic astrology, none are more misunderstood, feared, and exploited than Rahu (the North Node) and Ketu (the South Node). The Rahu Ketu and Kaal Sarpa Consultation at Shambhavaa is designed to demystify these shadowy planets. We aim to strip away the fear-mongering and provide you with a clear, logical, and actionable understanding of your deepest karmic axis.

Rahu represents our unfulfilled worldly desires, our obsessions, and where we must expand in this lifetime. Ketu represents our past life expertise, our innate spiritual wisdom, and where we seek detachment and liberation. Together, they form the evolutionary axis of your soul.

Who This Consultation Is For

This reading is specifically for individuals who:

  • Have been told they have a "Kaal Sarpa Dosha" and want an honest, non-fear-based assessment of what it actually means.
  • Are running a Rahu or Ketu Mahadasha/Antardasha and are experiencing sudden upheaval, confusion, or intense material/spiritual shifts.
  • Feel an obsessive, unquenchable desire in one area of life (Rahu) and a deep, inexplicable apathy in another (Ketu).
  • Are currently experiencing the intense Rahu-Ketu nodal return or nodal opposition transits.

What We Check: The Karmic Axis

During this consultation, we intensely focus on the houses and signs occupied by the lunar nodes in your D1 chart:

  • The Rahu Placement: Where Rahu sits is where you are forced to learn, experiment, and break boundaries. It is an area of high illusion but also high material potential.
  • The Ketu Placement: Where Ketu sits is an area you have already mastered in the past. It often feels like a "black hole" where you lack attachment, or where things are suddenly taken away to force spiritual growth.
  • Dispositors (Rulers): Rahu and Ketu do not own signs. They act like the planets that rule the signs they sit in. We analyze their dispositors to understand how these nodes will actually behave in your life.

The Reality of Kaal Sarpa

If all seven visible planets are hemmed between Rahu and Ketu in your chart, a Kaal Sarpa formation is created.

Unfortunately, this formation is often used to scare clients into believing their life is cursed. At Shambhavaa, we view Kaal Sarpa differently. It represents an intense, concentrated focus of energy that can lead to extremes—extreme struggles, but often, extreme success and unique destiny. We will map out exactly how this formation operates in your chart and the age at which its restrictive energies tend to break.

Dasha Activation and Transits

Rahu and Ketu periods are notorious for bringing sudden, unexpected changes—both positive and negative.

If you are entering a nodal Dasha, we will break down the timeline, explaining the psychological storms and material opportunities it will bring. We also look at the current transits of Rahu and Ketu (which change signs every 18 months) to see where the eclipses are triggering changes in your life right now.

(For general timing questions unrelated to the nodes, see our dasha and transit reading).

What You Need to Provide

  1. Date of Birth
  2. Exact Time of Birth
  3. Place of Birth

What Is Not Promised

  • We do not sell expensive Kaal Sarpa poojas. If remedies are required, we suggest traditional mantras, disciplines, and lifestyle changes that you can perform yourself.
  • We do not use the nodes to scare you. A Rahu dasha can bring immense worldly success, and a Ketu dasha can bring profound peace if navigated correctly.
  • We do not promise that astrological remedies will entirely stop a nodal transit from doing its karmic job.
