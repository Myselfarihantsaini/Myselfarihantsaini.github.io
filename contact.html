How to Book a Consultation

To ensure a smooth and efficient booking process, we handle all consultation requests and scheduling directly via WhatsApp.

Step 1: Click the booking link below to open a direct WhatsApp chat with our team. Step 2: Let us know which consultation category you are interested in. Step 3: Provide your complete and accurate birth details (see below). Step 4: Once your details are confirmed and payment is completed, we will schedule a mutually convenient time for your consultation call.

Required Birth Details

Vedic astrology requires precise mathematical calculations to erect an accurate birth chart (Kundli). To ensure the highest level of accuracy during your reading, you must provide the following information when booking:

  1. Date of Birth: (e.g., 15 August 1990)
  2. Exact Time of Birth: (e.g., 04:35 PM)
Note: If you are unsure of your exact birth time, please inform us beforehand. Inaccurate birth times can drastically change divisional charts and dasha timing.
  1. Place of Birth: City, State, and Country (e.g., New Delhi, India)
