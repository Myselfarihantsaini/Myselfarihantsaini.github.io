# Shambhavaa.com AdSense Audit

## Overview
Current AdSense Status: Needs attention (Low value content)
Ads.txt Status: Authorized
Goal: Improve shambhavaa.com from a thin-content site to a high-value, comprehensive consultation platform ready for AdSense approval.

## Audit Findings

### 1. Homepage completeness
- **URL**: `/`
- **Issue**: Homepage serves as a single-page landing site for services with anchor links to WhatsApp instead of driving traffic to informative, high-value service pages.
- **Severity**: High
- **Recommended fix**: Redesign homepage to summarize services and link to dedicated service pages. Add "How Consultation Works" and "What We Check" sections.
- **Action**: Manual action required.

### 2. Navigation menu
- **URL**: Global navigation
- **Issue**: Missing links to dedicated service pages and FAQ.
- **Severity**: Medium
- **Recommended fix**: Update navigation to include: Home, About, Consultations (dropdown for Marriage, Career, Dasha & Transit, Remedies), FAQ, Contact.
- **Action**: Manual action required.

### 3. Footer links
