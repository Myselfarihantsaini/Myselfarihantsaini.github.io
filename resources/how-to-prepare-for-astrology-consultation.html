How to Prepare for an Astrology Consultation

Published by Shambhavaa | Updated September 9, 2026 | Editorial policy

You can arrive with a single question. There is no need to prepare a perfect life history or understand every chart symbol. A few reliable details and a clear idea of what you want to discuss make the conversation easier.

Write down the source of your birth time

Record your birth date, birthplace, and local time as originally recorded. Add where the time came from: a hospital document, another written record, or a relative's memory. If it is approximate, give a range. Keep the original local time instead of converting it yourself to another timezone.

If the town name has changed or several places share its name, include the state and country. For an overseas birth, check whether the calculator handles the timezone and daylight-saving rules for that date.

Turn a broad worry into a question

  • Instead of "Tell me everything about my career," try "I am considering a job change this year. What would this reading help me reflect on?"
  • Instead of "When will I marry?", try "I want to discuss relationships and timing. How much does the answer depend on my uncertain birth time?"
  • Instead of "Which remedy will fix my life?", try "What is the reason for this suggestion, what would it cost, and can I decline it?"

These are examples, not questions you have to copy. Choose one priority and two follow-ups so the important part does not get lost.

A short preparation note

  • My main question and any real deadline.
  • My birth details, their source, and what is uncertain.
  • Relevant background I am comfortable sharing.
  • What I want clarified before booking: fee, length, format, follow-up, and cancellation terms.

For birth-time rectification, dated events may be requested. Separate documented dates from remembered ones. Do not add details merely because they seem to fit a reading. Share another person's birth details only with their permission.

During and after the conversation

Ask for unfamiliar terms to be explained. If you would like to record the session, agree that first. At the end, repeat back the main points and ask which conclusions are tentative. You do not have to make a purchase or an important life decision during the call.

Keep your notes private, especially when they contain family or relationship information. Use the consultation process page to understand booking, and read the cancellation terms before paying.
