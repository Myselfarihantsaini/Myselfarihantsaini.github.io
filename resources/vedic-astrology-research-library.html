Vedic Astrology Reading Guide

Published by Shambhavaa | Updated September 9, 2026 | Editorial policy

Start with the question you want to understand. This guide points to Shambhavaa's introductory articles and suggests how to compare explanations as you read. It is a reading map, not a collection of downloadable books or a claim that private notes have been independently verified.

If you are new to charts

Begin with how a kundli reading works. Learn the difference between calculating a position and interpreting it. Keep a short glossary for D1, ascendant, divisional chart, dasha, and transit; ask for explanations that do more than repeat those terms.

If your question is about work

Read the career framework, followed by D10 and birth-time uncertainty. Compare any interpretation with your actual skills, options, and constraints.

If your question is about relationships

Read what Navamsa adds and what a timing window means. Notice whether an explanation leaves room for consent, personal choice, and disagreement.

If you are comparing predictions

The dasha and transit guide gives you a checklist for comparing methods. Record the claim before the event and include assumptions, not just a striking sentence.

Keep a source note

When studying a book, record the title, author or translator, edition, page, and the rule being discussed. A book title alone does not support a specific claim. Different translations and schools may disagree. Distinguish a quotation from a later interpretation, and avoid presenting an illustrative chart as a real client case.
