Vedic Astrology Research Library

A responsible metadata-only study guide for connecting classical Jyotish topics with full-chart consultation.

The Shambhavaa research library integrates an offline astrology-books database into the website in a safe and useful way. The purpose is not to publish or redistribute books. Instead, this page turns the database into a clean study index that helps readers understand which areas of Jyotish support different consultation topics.

No PDF, DOC, chart file, private file path, or book text is hosted here. The index uses metadata only: broad categories, reference titles, and topic groupings. This keeps the website focused on original consultation guidance while still showing the study foundation behind Shambhavaa's approach.

What Was Integrated

  • 1461 local files were inspected as metadata.
  • 223 research-oriented PDF, DOC, HTML, and PPTX records were separated from chart files.
  • 1229 chart or birth-detail files were excluded from public use.
  • The public website receives only a topic index and selected reference titles.
  • The full files remain offline and are not redistributed through shambhavaa.com.

How to Use This Library

Astrology books can become confusing when they are read as isolated rules. One text may describe a yoga, another may discuss dasha timing, another may explain a divisional chart, and another may focus on remedies. A consultation has to bring these pieces together. At Shambhavaa, a reading begins with D1 chart promise, then checks relevant divisional charts, dasha, and transit before giving guidance.

This library is therefore arranged by consultation use, not just by book folder. A client looking at marriage timing should not jump straight to a single rule about Venus or the 7th house. The better study path is D1 marriage promise, Navamsa confirmation, dasha activation, transit support, and practical relationship context. The same principle applies to career, remedies, Rahu-Ketu, nakshatra, and general kundli reading.

Ethical Limits

This library is a study index, not a promise of results. Shambhavaa does not use astrology to guarantee marriage, job placement, wealth, healing, legal victory, or fixed outcomes. Astrology is used for self-understanding, timing awareness, spiritual reflection, and better preparation.

Medical, legal, financial, psychological, and emergency matters should always be handled with qualified professionals. Remedies, when suggested, are treated as spiritual and traditional supports after full chart analysis.
