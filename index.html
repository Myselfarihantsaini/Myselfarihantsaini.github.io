How Consultation Works

Full Chart First, Then Prediction

Accurate guidance is not made from one planet or one transit. Every reading begins with the D1 chart, then checks relevant divisional charts, dasha, and current transit.

Step 1

Birth Details

Share date, exact time, and place of birth so the Lagna, divisional charts, and dasha timeline can be calculated properly.

Step 2

Chart Analysis

The D1 chart shows the main promise. D9, D10, and other divisional charts confirm marriage, career, and specific life themes.

Step 3

Timing and Guidance

Mahadasha, antardasha, and transit are used for timing. Remedies are suggested only when the full chart supports them.
