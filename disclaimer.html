Astrology Disclaimer

Last Updated: May 2026

The services, readings, and educational content provided by Shambhavaa are strictly based on the traditional principles of Vedic Astrology (Jyotish). By accessing this site or booking a consultation, you acknowledge and agree to the following disclaimers:

1. Astrology is for Guidance Only

Astrology is a spiritual and analytical tool designed to provide self-reflection, understanding, and directional guidance. It is not an exact science. Predictions and chart readings are interpretations made by the astrologer based on the birth details provided. There are no absolute guarantees regarding the occurrence of specific events, timelines, or life outcomes.

2. Not a Substitute for Professional Advice

The astrological guidance provided by Shambhavaa must never be used as a replacement for professional counsel. Our readings do not substitute for:
  • Medical Advice: We do not diagnose, treat, or cure physical or psychological illnesses. For health concerns, always consult a qualified medical professional.
  • Legal Advice: We do not provide legal counsel. For legal issues, consult an attorney.
  • Financial/Business Advice: Astrological timing for business or investments is strictly for informational purposes. Consult a financial advisor before making monetary decisions.

3. Remedies are Spiritual Supports, Not Guarantees

If an astrological remedy (such as a mantra, fasting protocol, donation, or gemstone) is recommended during a consultation, it is offered as a traditional spiritual practice. We absolutely do not guarantee that performing a remedy will bring wealth, secure a marriage, cure a disease, or manifest a specific desire. Remedies are intended to promote discipline, focus, and spiritual alignment.

4. Personal Responsibility and Free Will

Life is a combination of karmic patterns and free will. The astrologer highlights the potential patterns in the chart, but the user is solely responsible for their actions. Shambhavaa and its astrologers hold no liability for the decisions you make, the actions you take, or the consequences of those actions following a consultation.

5. Emotional and Psychological Readiness

Astrology can sometimes bring up deep emotional themes, karmic blockages, or challenging periods (such as Saturn transits or difficult Dashas). If you are currently dealing with severe anxiety, trauma, or a psychological crisis, we strongly advise seeking help from a licensed therapist rather than relying on an astrological consultation.

By proceeding with a consultation at Shambhavaa, you confirm that you have read, understood, and accepted this disclaimer in its entirety.
