Refund Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

This policy explains how cancellations, rescheduling, and refunds are handled for Shambhavaa consultations. Exact service details are confirmed before booking.

Before A Session Is Confirmed

If a payment is made before a final session time is confirmed and you decide not to proceed, contact us promptly at shambhavaa.reviews@gmail.com. We will review the case and respond with available options.

Rescheduling

One reasonable reschedule may be offered when notice is given before the agreed session time. Repeated missed sessions or very late cancellations may not be eligible for refund.

After Consultation Delivery

Because astrology consultations involve time, preparation, chart study, and personalized interpretation, completed sessions are generally not refundable. If there is a genuine service issue, we will review it fairly.

No Outcome-Based Refunds

Refunds are not based on whether a predicted trend, remedy, relationship, job, income event, or personal outcome occurs. Astrology guidance is interpretive and educational, not a guarantee.

Contact

For refund or cancellation questions, email shambhavaa.reviews@gmail.com.
