How a Shambhavaa Kundli Consultation Works

Booking an astrology consultation can sometimes feel daunting. At Shambhavaa, we believe in complete transparency about our process. We want you to know exactly what happens from the moment you book to the guidance you receive.

Here is a step-by-step breakdown of how a Vedic Kundli reading works with us.

Step 1: Providing Your Birth Details

Precision is the foundation of Vedic astrology. When you reach out to book a reading, we will ask for three critical pieces of information:
  • Date of Birth
  • Exact Time of Birth (Accurate to the minute, if possible)
  • Place of Birth (City, State, Country)

Why is time so important? The Ascendant (Lagna) changes roughly every two hours, but divisional charts (like the Navamsa or Dasamsa) change in a matter of minutes. An accurate birth time is vital for an accurate reading.

Step 2: The Astrologer's Preparation

Once your booking is confirmed, the reading begins long before we get on a call. We do not use automated reports. The astrologer sits with your chart to manually analyze the data:
  • The D1 Chart (Rasi Chart): We assess the natal promise. What are the natural strengths, blockages, and core life themes encoded in your birth chart?
  • Relevant Divisional Charts: If you booked a career reading, we look deeply at the D10 (Dasamsa). If it’s a marriage reading, we analyze the D9 (Navamsa). This confirms the strength of the D1 chart.
  • Dasha and Transit Alignment: A promise in the chart only happens when the timing is right. We map out your Mahadasha (major planetary period) and current planetary transits (Gochar) to understand exactly where you are on your timeline.

Step 3: The Consultation Session

At the scheduled time, we connect via WhatsApp or call. The session is conversational, structured, and focused on clarity.
  • Chart Validation: We may ask a few past-event questions to verify that the chart accurately reflects your life.
  • Direct Answers: We address the specific questions you booked the reading for—whether it's career timing, marriage delays, or general life direction.
  • No Fear-Mongering: We explain planetary placements logically. If there is a difficult transit (like Sade Sati), we explain its purpose rather than creating fear.

Step 4: Final Guidance and Remedies

Astrology is not just about prediction; it is about navigation. If your chart indicates a difficult period or a specific blockage, we will suggest practical, traditional remedies.
  • These may include mantra chanting, lifestyle disciplines, or donations based on your chart’s needs.
  • What we do not do: We do not guarantee that a remedy will magically fix a problem, nor do we push expensive rituals. Remedies are spiritual tools for alignment.

Step 5: Post-Consultation

After the session, you are encouraged to take notes. You walk away with a clearer understanding of your karmic timeline, the energies currently active in your life, and the actions required to navigate them responsibly.
